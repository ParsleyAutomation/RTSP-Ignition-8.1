1) MediaMTX
   Role:      Bundled gateway sidecar that pulls each camera's RTSP(S) feed and
              remuxes it to HLS and WebRTC (no transcoding). Its HTTP ports are bound to
              loopback; the WebRTC media UDP port is bound on all interfaces.

   License:   MIT  ->  mediamtx-LICENSE-MIT.txt
